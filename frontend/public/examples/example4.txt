Mr. Smith is a 68-year-old male with a past medical history significant for type 2 diabetes mellitus (diagnosed 2011), hypertension, chronic kidney disease stage IIIb (baseline creatinine 1.8 mg/dL), coronary artery disease status post percutaneous coronary intervention with drug-eluting stent to the LAD in June 2021, hyperlipidemia, and paroxysmal atrial fibrillation on apixaban, who presented to the Emergency Department on November 2, 2024 with progressive dyspnea on exertion over the preceding 48 hours and substernal chest pressure radiating to the left arm.

The patient reports that 3 days prior to admission he noticed increased swelling in his bilateral lower extremities and orthopnea requiring 3 pillows to sleep. He admits to running out of furosemide approximately 1 week ago. He also reports a productive cough with white sputum for the past 5 days. He denies fever, chills, hemoptysis, syncope, or palpitations. He experienced a similar episode 18 months ago which required hospitalization for acute decompensated heart failure.

In the ED, initial vitals were: T 37.2C, HR 112 bpm (irregular), BP 168/94 mmHg, RR 28 breaths/min, SpO2 88% on room air improving to 94% on 4L nasal cannula. ECG showed atrial fibrillation with rapid ventricular response, left ventricular hypertrophy pattern, and nonspecific ST-T wave changes. Initial troponin-T was 0.08 ng/mL (repeat at 6 hours: 0.07 ng/mL). BNP was 2,840 pg/mL. Chest X-ray demonstrated bilateral pleural effusions and pulmonary edema. He received IV furosemide 80 mg and IV diltiazem 20 mg.

Past medical history includes heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (last echo March 2024: EF 35%), obstructive sleep apnea on home CPAP, gout, and benign prostatic hyperplasia. Allergic to ACE inhibitors (angioedema).

Home medications: apixaban 5 mg BID, metoprolol succinate 100 mg daily, furosemide 40 mg daily, losartan 100 mg daily, atorvastatin 80 mg daily, metformin 1000 mg BID, empagliflozin 10 mg daily, insulin glargine 22 units at bedtime, aspirin 81 mg daily, allopurinol 200 mg daily, tamsulosin 0.4 mg at bedtime.

During hospitalization, echocardiogram revealed worsening EF to 30% with moderate mitral and tricuspid regurgitation. Right-sided thoracentesis on November 6 removed 1,200 mL of transudative fluid. CT angiography showed no pulmonary embolism. Renal ultrasound showed chronic medical renal disease. Creatinine peaked at 2.6 mg/dL on hospital day 3 and improved to 1.9 mg/dL by discharge on November 14, 2024. HbA1c was 8.2%.

At discharge, metoprolol was increased to 150 mg daily, furosemide changed to 60 mg BID, sacubitril-valsartan 24/12 mg BID was initiated (replacing losartan), spironolactone 25 mg daily was added, insulin glargine increased to 26 units, and insulin lispro 4 units with meals was started.
